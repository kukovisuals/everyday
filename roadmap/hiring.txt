Here’s a tight checklist of shader types that make a Meta/Reality Labs recruiter think “ready for XR production.” Aim for 6–8 polished pieces total.

**1) Core real-time lighting (PBR)**

* GGX + IBL, punctual lights, shadowing, specular AA, cheap clearcoat/aniso variants.
* Mobile-ready versions with packed masks (R: metal, G: rough, B: AO).

**2) Fabric/skin “hero” materials**

* Thin-layer SSS (wrap lighting/thickness map) and anisotropic fabric (warp/weft).
* Great for wearables—show tiling control + mip/normal filtering done right.

**3) Transparent & water**

* Order-independent-ish transparency workaround, refraction, Fresnel, foam/rim.
* Show a cheap VR-safe water (no heavy screen gathers).

**4) Volumetrics & fog**

* Height fog, light shafts, cheap volumetric scattering; demonstrate temporal stability.

**5) NPR/Stylistic**

* Toon/matcap/cel with banding AA, outlines (Sobel or depth/normal-based) and ramp control.

**6) Procedural/SDF modeling**

* SDF blend ops (smin), AO/soft shadows, curvature; one organic piece (your capsule-coral is perfect).
* Stress test with repetition + your AA fixes (FXAA/SMAA + fwidth tricks).

**7) Particles & flow fields**

* GPU-friendly update (vertex/TF or texture buffers), curl noise advection, signed distance collisions.
* One “biology/organisms” VFX fits your style.

**8) Deformation & shading in the vertex stage**

* Skinning/morphs, wind/bend, distance-based LOD swaps with stable shading.

**9) Post-process fundamentals**

* Tonemapping (ACES), bloom, color grading LUT, FXAA/SMAA; explain why TAA is tricky in VR.

**10) XR specifics (critical)**

* Single-pass stereo/multiview demo, fixed-foveated/variable rate shading aware materials.
* Mobile constraints: 90/120 Hz on Quest targets, texture/mem budgets, MSAA vs bandwidth tradeoffs.

**11) Environment shaders**

* Triplanar with world-space UVs, detail normal/roughness, baked light probe support.
* Dithered alpha for foliage (Temporal-stable pattern).

**12) Tools & debug views (small but pro)**

* Albedo/rough/metal/normal isolators, UV checker, wireframe, overdraw heatmap.

**Presentation tips (make each piece “hireable”):**

* 30–60s capture + a 1-page breakdown: goals, key tricks, perf numbers (Quest-like), texture counts, keyword variants.
* Show **before/after** on aliasing, mip/normal filtering, and perf (draw calls, GPU time).
* Include GLSL source excerpts (readable), plus Unity/Three.js integration notes if relevant.
